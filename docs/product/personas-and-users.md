# Personas and users

## Primary

**Validation analyst**  
Works the human-review queue, inspects evidence, confirms mismatches, escalates amendments.

**Operations lead**  
Tracks volumes, auto-approve rates, amendment rates, and failure spikes.

## Secondary

**Rules owner / account ops**  
Maintains customer-specific validation rules.

**Implementing engineer**  
Owns pipeline reliability, evals, and observability.

**Assignment evaluator / reviewer**  
Needs reproducible demos, docs, and traces.

## Indirect (Part 2+)

**Shipper contact**  
Receives amendment requests / draft communications after human approval gates.
